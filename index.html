Tick a tail's checkbox to show or hide all its flights; tap the tail name to expand its flight list. Tap a flight name to zoom and jump the timeline to that day. Use the Flights pills under the slider to show only recent flights (zones always stay). Each active tail has its own color; less-active tails share grey. Dashed lime green = non-US aircraft (Guatemalan tails flying out of Tampico). Yellow = infested zone, blue = surveillance. Sources: Texas = TAHC; New Mexico = NM Livestock Board / NMSU map; Arizona = public Google My Map "New World Screwworm" (unofficial, no stated author). Small grey dots = flight start/end points. Red dots = confirmed cases (USDA), placed within the county (not exact locations); hollow red rings = fly-trap detections. Basemap switch is top-right.
